# 1. Ask

## Context

Part of Google Data Analytics Capstone Case Study 3 ("Follow Your Own Case Study Path"): acting
as a junior data analyst at a business intelligence consultancy, choosing a business question,
sourcing a fresh dataset, and delivering client-ready recommendations from scratch.

## Business task

**Client:** a (hypothetical) publishing house shaping its acquisition and marketing strategy.

**Task:** Using data on Amazon's top 50 bestselling books each year from 2009–2019, analyze what
characteristics — genre, price, rating, review count, author — are associated with a book
repeatedly making the bestseller list, to inform which kinds of books the client should acquire
and how to price/market them.

Guiding question: **What combination of genre, price, and reader reception (rating, review
count) characterizes repeat bestsellers, and has that pattern shifted over the 2009–2019 window?**

## Key factors / metrics

- Genre (fiction / nonfiction)
- Price
- User rating, number of reviews
- Author (repeat appearances)
- Year (for trend over the decade)

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing acquisitions team (client) | Decision-maker | Decides which manuscripts/authors to acquire |
| BI consultancy account lead | Internal | Reviews the analysis before client presentation |
| Marketing team | Downstream users | Would use pricing/genre findings to shape launch campaigns |

## Data source (planned)

[Amazon Top 50 Bestselling Books 2009–2019](https://www.kaggle.com/sootersaalu/amazon-top-50-bestselling-books-2009-2019)
(Kaggle, CC0, via Souter Saalu) — full source evaluation in the Prepare phase.

## Audience & presentation

Primary audience: the publishing client's acquisitions and marketing leads — non-technical,
decision-focused. Deliverable: a concise written report plus visualizations showing which
genre/price/rating combinations characterize repeat bestsellers.
