# 1. Ask

## Business task

Bellabeat is a high-tech manufacturer of health-focused smart products for women (the Leaf
tracker, the Time watch, the Spring water bottle, the app, and a subscription membership).
Cofounder and Chief Creative Officer Urška Sršen believes that analyzing smart device fitness
data — beyond Bellabeat's own — could reveal growth opportunities for the company.

**Task:** Analyze smart device usage data from non-Bellabeat users (the public FitBit Fitness
Tracker dataset) to answer:

> What are some trends in smart device usage, how could these trends apply to Bellabeat
> customers, and how could they inform Bellabeat's marketing strategy?

The analysis focuses on **one Bellabeat product** and produces high-level marketing
recommendations, presented to the Bellabeat executive team.

## Key stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Urška Sršen | Cofounder & Chief Creative Officer | Requested this analysis; owns the marketing/growth strategy |
| Sando Mur | Cofounder, mathematician, executive team | Reviews and approves recommendations |
| Bellabeat marketing analytics team | Peers | Uses the findings to build the broader marketing strategy |

## How this drives the business decision

Bellabeat currently has no large-scale usage dataset of its own comparable to this analysis's
source. Studying how people already use a comparable smart device (FitBit) in their daily lives —
activity, steps, sleep, heart rate — surfaces general smart-device usage patterns that can
reasonably be expected to transfer to Bellabeat's own users, and therefore inform which usage
patterns to design marketing messaging around for a chosen Bellabeat product.

## Note on scope vs. the Cyclistic case study

Unlike the Cyclistic case study (one company's own operational data), this analysis uses a
**third-party public dataset** (FitBit users, not Bellabeat users) as a proxy to infer general
smart-device usage behavior. That distinction — and its limitations — is addressed explicitly in
the Prepare phase (data credibility, ROCCC).
