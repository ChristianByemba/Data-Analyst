# 1. Ask

## Context

Part of Google Data Analytics Capstone Case Study 3 ("Follow Your Own Case Study Path"): acting
as a junior data analyst at a business intelligence consultancy, choosing a business question,
sourcing a fresh dataset, and delivering client-ready recommendations from scratch.

## Business task

**Client:** a (hypothetical) US grocery retail chain reassessing its avocado pricing and
purchasing strategy.

**Task:** Using historical US avocado price and volume data, analyze how price varies by region,
season, and type (conventional vs. organic), and how price relates to volume sold, to inform the
client's pricing and inventory decisions.

Guiding question: **How do avocado prices and sales volume vary by region, season, and type, and
what does the price–volume relationship suggest about demand sensitivity to price?**

## Key factors / metrics

- Average price, total volume sold (the core variables)
- Type (conventional / organic)
- Region
- Date (for seasonality and year-over-year trend)

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Retail category manager (client) | Decision-maker | Sets pricing/purchasing strategy for avocados |
| BI consultancy account lead | Internal | Reviews the analysis before client presentation |
| Regional store operations | Downstream users | Would act on region-specific pricing/inventory guidance |

## Data source (planned)

[Avocado Prices](https://www.kaggle.com/neuromusic/avocado-prices) (Kaggle, CC0, via Justin
Kiggins / the Hass Avocado Board) — full source evaluation in the Prepare phase.

## Audience & presentation

Primary audience: the retail client's category manager and regional ops leads — non-technical,
decision-focused. Deliverable: a concise written report plus visualizations showing regional and
seasonal price/volume patterns clearly enough to inform a pricing decision.
