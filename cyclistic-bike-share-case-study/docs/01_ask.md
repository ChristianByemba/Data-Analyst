# 1. Ask

## Business task

Cyclistic is a bike-share company operating in Chicago (5,824 bikes, 692 stations). It offers
three pricing plans: single-ride passes, full-day passes, and annual memberships. Riders who buy
single-ride or full-day passes are **casual riders**; riders with an annual membership are
**members**.

Cyclistic's finance team has found that annual members are significantly more profitable than
casual riders. The director of marketing, Lily Moreno, believes the company's future growth
depends on maximizing annual memberships, and that the most efficient path there is converting
existing casual riders — who already know and use Cyclistic — rather than acquiring entirely new
customers.

**Task:** Analyze the last 12 months of Cyclistic trip data to answer:

> How do annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently?

The findings will be used to shape a marketing campaign aimed at converting casual riders into
annual members, and must be backed by clear, professional data visualizations to be approved by
the Cyclistic executive team.

## Key stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest |
|---|---|---|
| Lily Moreno | Director of Marketing | Owns the campaign strategy; assigned this specific question |
| Cyclistic marketing analytics team | Peers | Will use this analysis for the wider campaign (also covering *why* casual riders would convert, and *how* digital media can influence them) |
| Cyclistic executive team | Final approval | Detail-oriented; needs compelling, well-supported recommendations to approve the marketing program |

## How this drives the business decision

The marketing team cannot design an effective conversion campaign without first knowing what
actually differs in *behavior* between the two rider types (trip duration, frequency, day/time
patterns, seasonality, bike type, stations used, etc.). This analysis produces that evidence base,
which downstream informs messaging, targeting, and channel choices for the campaign — the next two
questions in the broader program (not in scope here).
